Projects

Land Registration Management Information System

LRMIS (Land Registration Management Information System) is a web-based land registration workflow system built with a **FastAPI backend, React frontend, and MongoDB database**. The system streamlines the land registration process by allowing users to submit land applications, manage applicant profiles, track documents, comments, and objections, and monitor workflow transitions. It also supports surveyor assignment, survey milestones, survey reports, and registrar reviews, while providing **analytics dashboard endpoints and live parcel map geofeeds** to improve decision-making, transparency, and overall management of land registration activities.

Neuro Lens, Machine Detection for Brain Hemorrhage and Glioma tumor.(Graduation project)

Accurate and timely diagnosis is critical in medical fields, yet the diagnostic process remains vulnerable to human error, particularly in the evaluation of neurological conditions. Our project proposal for the development of an Intelligent Diagnostic System designed to assist doctors and radiologists in the diagnosis of brain tumors : brain glioma tumors (using MRI) and intracranial hemorrhages (ICH) (using CT scans). The system will use the deep learning models, specifically variants of the CNN architecture, to not only classify the presence of disease but also to accurately identify and segment the affected regions. The primary objectives are to enhance the efficiency and reliability of medical evaluations, minimize the risk of diagnostic errors, and provide a transparent, user-friendly platform for all users, including patients. By integrating advanced AI with clinical workflow, this project aims to reduce the workload on medical professionals and ultimately improve patient care outcomes.
